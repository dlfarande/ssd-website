About Us

A proprietorship built on reliable supply and hands-on engineering

Established in 2017 as a proprietorship firm, SSD Enterprises has grown into a trusted wholesale trader and engineering solutions provider based in Wagholi, Pune. The firm specialises in industrial automation and instrumentation components — temperature controllers, inductive sensors, digital timers, relays, meters and totalisers — sourced from established brands such as Selec and ifm.

Beyond supplying spares off the shelf, SSD Enterprises takes on turnkey engineering projects and IoT-enabled software solutions, helping manufacturing and process industries move from individual components to complete, working systems. The business is led by proprietor P Farande and operates as a retail and works-contract business registered under GST.
